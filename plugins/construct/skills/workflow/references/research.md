# Research

Research only a named uncertainty: a specific library behavior, version, or constraint the work depends on. 

- A real open product decision gets one focused question, then the turn stops and waits.
- An unproven high-stakes premise (the whole plan depends on it) gets one read-only challenge before work starts.
- Read-only: research authorizes no write. Name a missing tool instead of guessing.
- Evidence cites a URL or `path:line` and separates facts from assumptions.
- Persist findings only when asked: in the feature document Log, or `construct-memory save`.
